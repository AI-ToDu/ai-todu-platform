This version uses a Fake REST API. Production services, commercial subscriptions and fiscal integration are future work; they are not available in this demo.

Terms & privacy

This prototype is for university evaluation. Use fictional data only. Demo records are stored in your browser or the local demo server. No real payments or fiscal documents are issued. Browser records can be erased by clearing site data. The interface is not a production service and does not provide production identity or security guarantees. External GitHub links open third-party services under their own terms. This page does not submit contact messages or collect analytics.
